About Us

It all started with my personal experiences…

Augety was born from a personal journey – our founder’s struggle.

I stood in front of the mirror, trying on yet another outfit that just didn’t feel right. The fit was bad, the fabric wasn’t what I wanted, while the perfect pieces always came with an excessive price tag. I kept asking myself – why was it so hard to find clothes that were both flattering and high-quality without being overpriced?

That moment sparked something in me. Instead of settling for what was available, I decided to create what was missing. In August 2020, a journey with AUGETY started – a fashion brand that’s well-structured, thoughtfully designed, and committed to offering high-quality, figure-flattering pieces at a price that is more accessible.

High-quality fashion, made more accessible

At Augety, we believe that high-quality fashion should not be an unattainable luxury. That’s why each of our pieces is thoughtfully designed, from fabric selection to fit and functionality, ensuring you never have to compromise between quality, style, and affordability.

After almost five years in the game, we’ve been constantly evolving to give our customers the best experience. From enhancing product quality to optimizing our operations, we strive for excellence and innovation every day. We are deeply grateful for the trust and support of our customers – both in Vietnam and around the world – we’ve fulfilled over 200,000 orders (and counting!).

If you’re someone who often buys activewear from international brands, you’ll notice that Augety’s active line are just as premium, but sometimes even “superior”. That’s because our materials are exclusively manufactured by overseas top-tier factories following our strict quality standards. Yet, our pricing is only 1/5 of what you’d pay for big-name brands.

“From active to lifestyle”



Fashion is more than just clothing, it’s a lifestyle!. Augety want to inspire a healthier, more balanced life, both physically and mentally. Our designs are made to move with you, whether you’re hitting the gym, doing yoga, or just hanging out with friends. With Augety, your outfit is effortlessly versatile. You can go straight from a workout to a café without missing a thing.

We’re building a community of “that girls” – girls who glow from the inside out, who love themselves, love life, and radiate confidence and energy. Because fashion is not just about what you wear, it’s a statement of how you live.

Are you ready to step into your best self with Augety?
